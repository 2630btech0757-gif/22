Complete Text Alignment Demo

1. Justify (All Sides / Left & Right)

This paragraph uses text-align justify. It spaces out the words evenly so that both the left and right margins form straight, vertical edges. This style is frequently used in books, newspapers, and print magazines to keep the blocks of text looking uniform.

2. Left Aligned

This paragraph is aligned to the left margin, which creates a straight left side and a ragged right side. This is the default text direction for English and most other Western languages.

3. Right Aligned

This paragraph is aligned to the right margin, keeping the right edge straight while the left edge remains uneven and staggered.

4. Centered

This paragraph is centered evenly inside the wrapper container, leaving symmetrical space on both sides of every line.
