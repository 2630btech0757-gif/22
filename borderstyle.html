CSS Border Styles Demonstration

Solid: A flat, continuous line.
Dashed: A series of rectangular dashes.
Dotted: A series of round dots.
Double: Two parallel solid lines.
Groove: A carved 3D effect.
Ridge: A raised 3D effect.
Inset: Makes the box look embedded into the page.
Outset: Makes the box look raised out of the page.
None: No border visible (default behavior).

Shorthand Method

This box uses the single-line border shorthand and has rounded corners.
